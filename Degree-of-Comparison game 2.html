📘 Naik Level Pengetahuan

Tiga Level Perbandingan

Ketuk setiap kartu level untuk membuka contoh kalimatnya!

LEVEL 1

Positive Degree

tall

Membandingkan dua hal yang setara/sama: as + adjective + as.

Batik Bantul is as beautiful as batik from other regions.
LEVEL 2

Comparative Degree

taller

Membandingkan dua hal yang berbeda: adjective+er / more+adjective + than.

The pine forest is taller than the bamboo grove.
LEVEL 3

Superlative Degree

tallest

Membandingkan lebih dari dua hal, menunjuk paling: the + adjective+est / the most.

Puncak Becici is the highest spot in Dlingo.

🗺️ Latihan Terbimbing

Peta Petunjuk

Lengkapi kalimat berikut. Gunakan tombol petunjuk (hint) jika kamu butuh bantuan — setiap jawaban benar memberimu koin!

1Positive — The bamboo craft from Bantul is as ______ (skillful) as the batik craft. 🪙 +10
Petunjuk: pola as + adjective + as, tanpa perubahan bentuk kata.

2Comparative — Mount Merapi is ______ (high) than Bukit Panguk. 🪙 +10
Petunjuk: adjective satu suku kata → tambahkan -er.

3Superlative — Of all the traditional crafts, batik is ______ (popular) souvenir in Yogyakarta. 🪙 +10
Petunjuk: adjective 2 suku kata lebih → the most + adjective.

4Irregular — My English score this semester is ______ (good) than last semester. 🪙 +10
Petunjuk: good termasuk adjective tidak beraturan.
